# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the explicit brief: TypeScript, React, Vite and Three.js; static hosting with Vercel compatibility.

## Product Purpose

A cinematic introduction and functional laboratory for inspecting VIPER's real recorded robot behavior and evidence.

## Users

Research visitors exploring robot execution and researchers inspecting recorded telemetry, outcomes and provenance.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Browse genuine historical recordings without a GPU backend. Preserve all robotics, training, evaluation and protocol code. Separate historical classical results from current-revision qualification and unverified learned-policy claims. E4 is conversion infrastructure; E5 is not implemented. Work stays on a dedicated branch, with no automatic merge or deployment.

## Brand Commitments

VIPER. Graphite and charcoal, soft white and metallic gray, restrained ice-blue. FROM INTENT TO MOTION. Single-viewport homepage, real robot focal point, functional research workspace underneath.

## Accessibility

Responsive mobile support, keyboard controls, reduced motion, truthful loading and graphics failure states.
